Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Investor Questions

Clear, direct answers to common questions about mutual funds, AMFI regulations, account opening, and security.

🔍
What is the official role of an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor? +
An AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN holder) is authorized by the Association of Mutual Funds in India to distribute mutual fund schemes, assist investors with KYC documentation, explain scheme features from official SID/KIM documents, and facilitate paperless transactions through regulated platforms. Distributors do not provide discretionary portfolio management or guaranteed profit promises.
What are the AMFI registration details for Radhe Investments? +
Radhe Investments operates under AMFI Registration Number ARN-25045 and EUIN: E043968, held by Nikhil Sukhadia. Investors can verify this registration directly on the official AMFI India portal.
Does Radhe Investments guarantee returns or provide speculative stock tips? +
Strictly No. In accordance with SEBI and AMFI regulatory frameworks, market-linked investments carry inherent market risks and no entity can legally assure or guarantee returns. Radhe Investments provides investor education and distribution support; we strictly do not operate tip channels or promise fixed yields.
How does a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) work? +
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly (monthly or quarterly) into a chosen mutual fund scheme. SIP enforces financial discipline, removes the psychological stress of market timing, and leverages Rupee Cost Averaging by buying more units when prices dip.
What documents are required to start a SIP or open a Demat account? +
Standard KYC requirements include: (1) PAN card copy, (2) Aadhaar card linked to active mobile for OTP validation, (3) Bank account details (cancelled cheque or latest bank statement with IFSC), (4) Passport-size photograph, and (5) Email address. Never share your net banking passwords or trading credentials with anyone.
How are my investment funds handled? Does Radhe Investments hold my money? +
No. Radhe Investments never holds or deposits investor funds into any private account. All investments are routed directly via official banking channels (NACH mandates, net banking, UPI) to the respective Asset Management Company (AMC) or authorized clearing corporation (NSE/BSE). Units are registered directly in your name under your PAN.
