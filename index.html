FAQs

Common questions, clear answers.

Does this website provide personalised investment advice?

No personalised advice is offered through this website. The content is general information only. Any regulated service or recommendation must be provided only by an appropriately authorised/registered person within their permitted scope.

Can anyone guarantee returns from shares or mutual funds?

No. Market-linked investments carry risk and returns are not guaranteed. Be cautious of claims of assured profits, risk-free trading or pressure to act quickly.

Will my account open automatically if I contact you?

No. Any account application is subject to the official provider's eligibility checks, KYC, documentation, terms and approval. Contacting Radhe Investments does not guarantee acceptance.

Should I share an OTP or password for assistance?

No. Never share your OTP, password, PIN, trading login, bank credentials or other security information with anyone. Use official channels for account access.

Is Radhe Investments or this website SEBI-approved?

This template does not claim that Radhe Investments or this website is SEBI-approved or independently SEBI-registered. Verify the relevant intermediary, its current status, registration details and the precise permitted role directly through official sources before proceeding.

Where can I find official investor information?

Visit the SEBI Investor website and the relevant provider's official website. Read all applicable documents and risk disclosures.
